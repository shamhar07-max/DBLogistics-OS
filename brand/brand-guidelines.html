01

Logo

The master logo is used exactly as generated — never redrawn, recoloured, re-typeset or cropped inside its lockup. The file is a transparent PNG with near-black ink, so it always travels on a pure-white “plate”, like a label on a container door.

Master lockup · digitalburj-logistics-os-logo.png (1983 × 793). Clear space = height of the “O” in LOGISTICS on every side.

Hexagon mark · favicon, app icon, avatar. Pixel-crop of the master, nothing redrawn.

Icon sizes · 512 · 192 · 180 · 64 · 32 · ico

Minimum size & placement

Lockup: ≥ 160 px wide on screen · 35 mm in print. Below that, use the mark. In the app, the logo sits top-left on a white plate (radius 10 px) over container-green — see the sidebar. Only the empty white margin of the PNG may be trimmed; the artwork inside is never touched.

Don’t place the logo directly on colour or photos — use the white plate.

Don’t recolour the mark or wordmark.

Don’t stretch, rotate or add effects.

Don’t crop into the lockup or separate the tagline.

Data-display rules
  • Estimated vs actual is always visible — hatched = inferred.
  • Every figure drills to its source record; missing data is shown, never hidden.
  • Identifiers (BL, AWB, container, HS) always in IBM Plex Mono.
  • Money = AED by default, 2 dp, tabular numerals; currency shown on foreign amounts.

Responsive & field rules
  • Driver & warehouse mobile: 48 px touch targets, one-hand primary action, high-contrast amber/ink.
